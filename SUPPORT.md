# Support

`nodered-mcp` is a single-maintainer project. There is no formal support
channel, no Discord, and no paid tier. Below is what is available and what is
not.

## Where to ask

- **Bug reports and feature requests**: open a [GitHub issue][issues]. Use the
  bug or feature templates; the maintainer triages them as time allows.
- **Questions about your specific setup**: also a GitHub issue. Use the
  question title prefix (`question: `) and include the output of
  `nodered-mcp doctor --json` and your client configuration with secrets
  redacted.
- **Security issues**: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do **not** open a
  public issue.

## What to expect

- The maintainer works on this project in their own time. There is no SLA.
- A "good first issue" label means the maintainer would like to see a PR for
  it; it does not mean someone is available to mentor.
- Issues are not closed on a schedule. Stale issues stay open until the
  roadmap picks them up.

## What is not supported

- Real-time chat or calls.
- Custom builds, signed binaries for unsupported platforms, or private
  feature branches.
- Backports to release branches that have shipped.

[issues]: https://github.com/fgjcarlos/nodered-mcp/issues
